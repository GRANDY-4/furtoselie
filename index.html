Welcome to Furtoselie

The Kingdom of Furtoselie is a micronation founded upon the idea that even a small nation can have its own culture, identity, institutions, and community. Furtoselag is a nation where its citizens can build, create, and participate in something of their own.

🏰 Our Kingdom

Furtoselie is a semi-constitutional monarchy led by Ciftom, the Supreme Leader of the Kingdom. The Crown holds significant constitutional authority while representative institutions participate in the governance of the nation.

🌍 Our People

The people of Furtoselie form the heart of the nation. Community, friendship and cooperation are central to Furtoselag society.

🤝 Our Allies

Furtoselie believes in cooperation between micronations and maintains friendly relations with other nations and communities.

The Kingdom

The Kingdom of Furtoselie operates as a semi-constitutional monarchy. Ciftom serves as Supreme Leader and head of state, holding the highest authority within the Kingdom. Government institutions and representatives manage the everyday affairs of Furtoselie within the constitutional framework established by the Kingdom.

👑 Ciftom

Ciftom is the Supreme Leader of Furtoselie and the highest authority of the Kingdom. The Supreme Leader represents the nation and exercises the powers granted by the Constitution.

🏛️ Government

The government is responsible for the day-to-day administration of Furtoselie and operates alongside the authority of the Supreme Leader.

⚖️ Constitution

The Constitution defines the structure of the Kingdom, establishes the powers of Ciftom and the government, and provides the fundamental framework of Furtoselag law.
